IoT-Based Smart Distribution Transformer Monitoring System

An ESP32 prototype that monitors voltage, current and temperature of three transformer units, shows them on an LCD and the Blynk app, and trips a unit automatically when a limit is crossed.

View CAD DesignProject Report

Introduction

Distribution transformers step the grid voltage down for consumers. Overloading, abnormal voltage and high temperature can damage them, and checking them by hand only gives occasional readings.

This project builds a low-voltage demonstration of continuous monitoring. Three separate 230 V / 12 V, 3 A single-phase transformers represent the R, Y and B units. Sensors feed an ESP32, which displays the values on an LCD, sends them to the Blynk app, and switches off the affected unit when its current or temperature crosses the set limit.

Working Principle

  1. Sense: ZMPT101B modules read voltage and ACS712 modules read current of each unit. A DS18B20 sensor on each transformer reads temperature, and all three share one data pin.
  2. Process: The ESP32 converts the sensor signals into voltage, current and temperature values after calibration.
  3. Compare: Current is checked against 3.0 A and temperature against 50 °C for every unit separately.
  4. Act: If a limit is crossed, the relay of that unit cuts it off, its own cooling fan turns on, and a common buzzer sounds. Other units keep running.
  5. Display: Readings and status show on the 20×4 I2C LCD and are sent to the Blynk mobile dashboard, which also gives overload notifications.

Local LCD monitoring keeps working even if the internet is down. This is an educational prototype and not a certified protection relay.

Blynk Mobile Dashboard

The Blynk app shows the current and voltage of each phase, the temperature of the three transformers and the oil level. A reset button clears a tripped phase.

The live dashboard is private to the project owners and can be seen during the demonstration.
